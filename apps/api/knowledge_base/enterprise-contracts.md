# Enterprise Contracts and Renewals

## Contract status

Every Enterprise account has a contract status that controls what it can do:

- **Active**: the contract is within its term. All features, refunds and service credits are
  available.
- **Grace period**: the contract ended less than 30 days ago and renewal is being negotiated. All
  users keep full access, but seats cannot be added and new integrations cannot be enabled.
- **Expired**: the contract ended more than 30 days ago. The workspace becomes read-only, single
  sign-on keeps working, and refunds and service credits are unavailable until the customer
  renews.
- **Terminated**: the contract was ended early by either party under its terms. Data is retained
  for 90 days for export, then deleted.

## Renewals

Contracts renew automatically for the same term unless either party gives notice at least 60 days
before the renewal date. The account manager contacts the customer 90 days before renewal to
review seats and pricing. Price increases at renewal are capped at 7% unless the seat count or
plan changes.

## Adding seats mid-term

Seats can be added at any time while the contract is active. Added seats are charged on a prorated
basis until the end of the current term. Seats cannot be removed mid-term; the seat count can be
reduced at renewal.

## Who can approve changes

Contract changes, custom refunds above the support limit and pricing exceptions must be approved
by the customer's account manager. Support agents can explain the contract but cannot change its
terms.

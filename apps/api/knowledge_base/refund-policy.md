# Refund Policy

Driftwood wants every customer to be confident in their purchase. This policy explains when a
charge can be refunded, how much a support agent can refund without further approval, and how
long refunds take to arrive. Refund eligibility always depends on the customer's plan, the age of
the charge and the account's standing.

## General rules

These rules apply to every plan:

- Refunds are only issued for charges made in the last 60 days. Older charges cannot be refunded
  by support under any plan.
- A verified identity is required before any refund is discussed or issued. See the Account
  Security policy for how identity is verified.
- Refunds always go back to the original payment method. Driftwood never refunds to a different
  card, bank account or gift card.
- Refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on the customer's bank.
- Accounts suspended for violating the Terms of Service are not eligible for refunds.
- Usage overage charges (extra storage or API usage beyond the plan allowance) are not refundable.

## Free plan
<!-- audience: free -->

Free plan accounts have no subscription charges, so there is normally nothing to refund. The one
exception is an accidental upgrade: if a Free customer was charged for an upgrade they did not
intend, the full charge can be refunded within 7 days and the account is returned to the Free
plan. After 7 days the customer must cancel the upgrade instead, and the plan stays active until
the end of the paid period.

## Pro plan
<!-- audience: pro -->

### Monthly Pro subscriptions

Pro monthly charges can be refunded within 30 days of the charge date. A support agent may issue
a refund of up to $100 per request without further approval. Requests above $100 must be escalated
to the billing team, who respond within 2 business days.

Each Pro account is limited to one goodwill refund every 12 months. A goodwill refund is any
refund for a charge that was correctly applied (for example, a customer who forgot to cancel).
Refunds for billing errors made by Driftwood do not count toward this limit.

### Annual Pro subscriptions

Annual Pro subscriptions can be refunded within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date. The
refund is prorated for the unused months. The $100 agent limit applies to the prorated amount;
anything larger is escalated to the billing team. After 30 days, annual subscriptions are not
refundable but can be cancelled so they do not renew.

## Enterprise plan
<!-- audience: enterprise -->

Enterprise refunds follow the customer's signed contract first. Where the contract is silent, the
following applies:

- Support agents may issue a refund or service credit of up to $500 per request for charges in
  the last 60 days.
- Requests above $500 require approval from the customer's account manager and are handled as
  an escalation.
- Refunds are only available while the contract status is active or in its grace period. Accounts
  with an expired contract must renew before any refund can be processed.
- Credits for downtime are handled separately under the Service Credits policy.

## Non-refundable items

The following can never be refunded by support:

- Charges older than 60 days.
- Usage overage charges.
- Domain and custom-branding setup fees.
- Charges on accounts suspended for Terms of Service violations.
- Third-party marketplace add-ons, which are refunded by the add-on vendor.

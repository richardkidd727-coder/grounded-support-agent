# Uptime SLA and Service Credits

## Uptime commitment

Driftwood commits to 99.9% monthly uptime for Enterprise workspaces. Uptime is measured by
Driftwood's external monitoring and published on the status page. Scheduled maintenance announced
at least 72 hours in advance does not count as downtime.

## Credit amounts

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, the customer is entitled to a credit on their next
invoice:

| Monthly uptime | Credit |
|---|---|
| Below 99.9% but at least 99.0% | 10% of that month's fee |
| Below 99.0% but at least 95.0% | 25% of that month's fee |
| Below 95.0% | 50% of that month's fee |

## How to claim a credit

The customer must request the credit within 30 days of the end of the affected month, through
support or their success manager. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid out as
cash. Credits are only available while the contract is active or in its grace period.

## Exclusions

Credits do not apply to downtime caused by the customer's own network, third-party identity
providers, features labelled as beta, or force majeure events.

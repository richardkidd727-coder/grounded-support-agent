# Subscription Plans

Driftwood offers three plans: Free, Pro and Enterprise. All plans include the desktop and mobile
sync clients, the web workspace and end-to-end encrypted file transfer.

## Plan comparison

| Feature | Free | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $15 per user per month, or $144 per user per year | Custom annual contract |
| Users | Up to 3 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Storage | 5 GB total | 1 TB per user | Pooled, fair use |
| Maximum file size | 250 MB | 10 GB | 50 GB |
| Version history | 30 days | 180 days | 10 years |
| Support | Email, account and billing issues only | Email and live chat | 24/7 phone, chat and email with a named success manager |
| Uptime commitment | None | None | 99.9% SLA with service credits |
| Single sign-on (SAML) | No | No | Yes |
| API rate limit | 60 requests per minute | 600 requests per minute | 6,000 requests per minute |

## Free plan

The Free plan is for individuals and very small teams trying Driftwood. It has no time limit, but
storage and users are capped. Free accounts that stay inactive for 12 months are deleted after
two warning emails.

## Pro plan

The Pro plan is for growing teams. It is billed per user, monthly or annually. Annual billing saves
20% compared with monthly billing. Adding users mid-cycle is charged on a prorated basis on the
next invoice.

## Enterprise plan

The Enterprise plan is for organisations that need security controls, compliance and guaranteed
uptime. It is sold on an annual contract with a minimum of 50 seats. Enterprise includes SAML
single sign-on, audit logs, data residency in the EU or US, a 99.9% uptime SLA and a named
customer success manager.

## Changing plans

Customers can upgrade at any time from Settings > Billing. Upgrades take effect immediately and
are charged on a prorated basis. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.
Moving to or from Enterprise requires talking to the sales team.

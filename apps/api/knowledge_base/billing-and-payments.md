# Billing and Payments

## Accepted payment methods

Driftwood accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal for Pro subscriptions. Enterprise
customers can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice with 30-day payment terms. Prices are
charged in US dollars, euros or British pounds depending on the billing country.

## Invoices and receipts

Every charge produces an invoice that can be downloaded from Settings > Billing > Invoices.
Invoice numbers use the format INV-YYYY-NNNNN. Customers can add a company name, VAT number and
billing address to future invoices from the same page. Past invoices cannot be edited after they
are issued; support can issue a corrected copy for Pro and Enterprise accounts.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the subscription moves to the past due status and Driftwood retries the card
on days 1, 3, 7 and 14. The customer keeps full access during this period and receives an email
before each retry. If the payment still fails after 14 days, a Pro account is downgraded to the
Free plan. Files above the Free storage limit become read-only but are not deleted.

## Taxes

Sales tax, VAT or GST is added based on the billing address. Business customers in the EU and UK
can enter a valid VAT number to apply the reverse charge. Tax cannot be refunded once an invoice
has been filed, except where the tax was charged in error.

## Changing the billing email

The billing email receives invoices and payment notices. It can be changed by an account owner in
Settings > Billing, or by support after the customer's identity has been verified. The old address
receives a notification of the change.

## Proration

Upgrades and added seats are prorated to the day. The prorated amount appears on the next invoice
rather than as a separate charge. Removed seats are credited at the next renewal, not refunded.

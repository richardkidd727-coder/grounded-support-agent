# Support Hours and Response Times

## Support channels by plan

| Plan | Channels | Hours | First response target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | Email (account and billing issues only), community forum | Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm US Eastern | 3 business days |
| Pro | Email and live chat | Monday to Friday, 24 hours | 8 business hours |
| Enterprise | Phone, live chat and email, plus a named success manager | 24/7, every day of the year | Depends on priority (see below) |

The AI assistant is available to every plan at any time and can hand a conversation to a human
agent when needed.

## Free plan support
<!-- audience: free -->

Free plan customers can contact support by email about account access and billing only. How-to
questions are answered in the community forum and the help centre. Free plan tickets are handled
in the order they arrive.

## Enterprise priority levels
<!-- audience: enterprise -->

Enterprise tickets are assigned a priority that sets the response target:

| Priority | Meaning | First response |
|---|---|---|
| P1 Urgent | Service down or data loss for many users | 1 hour, 24/7 |
| P2 High | Major feature broken, no workaround | 4 hours, 24/7 |
| P3 Normal | Feature partly broken or a workaround exists | 1 business day |
| P4 Low | Questions and feature requests | 2 business days |

P1 issues are also paged to the on-call engineering team and the customer's success manager.

## Holidays

Free and Pro support is closed on US federal holidays. Enterprise support is never closed.

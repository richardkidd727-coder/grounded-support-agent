# Account Security and Identity Verification

## Verifying identity before account changes

Support must verify the customer's identity before discussing account-specific details or taking
any action on an account, including refunds, billing changes and account updates. A customer is
verified when they provide:

1. The email address on the account, and
2. One of: the last four digits of the card on file, or an invoice number from the last 12 months.

If verification fails three times in one conversation, support must stop and create an escalation
ticket for the security team. Support never asks for a full card number, a password or a
two-factor code.

## Two-factor authentication

Driftwood strongly recommends two-factor authentication (2FA) for every account. Enterprise
administrators can require it for all users. Supported methods are authenticator apps and hardware
security keys. SMS codes are not supported.

## Lost two-factor device

A customer who has lost their 2FA device should first use one of the recovery codes shown when 2FA
was enabled. If they have no recovery codes, support can reset 2FA after verifying identity. The
reset takes effect after a 24-hour security hold, and the account email is notified immediately.
Enterprise users must ask their workspace administrator instead.

## Suspicious activity

If a customer reports activity they do not recognise, support should advise them to change their
password, sign out all sessions from Settings > Security, and review the audit log. Possible
account compromise must always be escalated to the security team as a high priority ticket.

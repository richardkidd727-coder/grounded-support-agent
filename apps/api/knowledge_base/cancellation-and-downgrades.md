# Cancellation and Downgrades

## Cancelling a Pro subscription
<!-- audience: pro, free -->

Pro subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Cancellation
stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period. The account keeps Pro features
until the end of the period it has already paid for, then moves to the Free plan.

If the customer wants money back for the current period, the Refund Policy applies separately.
Cancelling does not automatically create a refund.

## Cancelling an Enterprise contract
<!-- audience: enterprise -->

Enterprise contracts cannot be cancelled mid-term unless the contract allows it. To stop renewal,
the customer must give written notice at least 60 days before the renewal date to their account
manager. Notice given later than 60 days before renewal means the contract renews for another
term.

## What happens after a downgrade

When an account moves to a plan with lower limits:

- Files above the new storage limit become read-only. Nothing is deleted.
- Users above the new user limit are suspended in reverse order of when they joined. Their files
  stay owned by the workspace.
- Version history older than the new plan's window is kept for 30 days, then removed.
- Features that the new plan does not include, such as single sign-on, are turned off immediately.

## Deleting an account

Deleting an account is permanent and different from cancelling. Only the account owner can delete
an account, from Settings > Account > Delete. Deleted data is retained for the period described in
the Data Export and Retention policy and then destroyed.

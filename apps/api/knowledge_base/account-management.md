# Account Management

## Details support can update

After verifying identity, support can update these account details on the customer's behalf:

- Billing email address
- Company name shown on invoices
- Contact phone number
- Email notification preferences (product updates, billing notices, security alerts)

Security alerts cannot be turned off for account owners.

## Details only the account owner can change

For security reasons, support cannot change the following. The account owner must change them
in Settings:

- The login email address
- The account password or two-factor settings
- The account owner role
- Workspace deletion

## Transferring account ownership

Ownership can be transferred by the current owner from Settings > Members. If the owner has left
the company and cannot be reached, a Pro or Enterprise customer can request a transfer through
support. This requires a signed request on company letterhead and takes 5 business days.

## Changing the workspace name

Workspace administrators can rename the workspace in Settings > Workspace. The workspace web
address changes only on Enterprise plans, by request to the success manager.

# Privacy and Personal Data Requests

## Personal data requests

Customers can ask Driftwood to access, correct or delete the personal data held about them, under
GDPR, UK GDPR and the CCPA. Requests are made by email to the privacy team or through support,
after identity verification. Driftwood responds within 30 days.

A deletion request removes the person's profile and personal data. Files they uploaded to a shared
workspace belong to the workspace owner and are not deleted unless the owner agrees.

## Data processing agreement

Enterprise customers receive a data processing agreement (DPA) as part of their contract. Pro
customers can accept the standard DPA online from Settings > Legal. Free accounts are covered by
the standard privacy policy only.

## Data residency
<!-- audience: enterprise -->

Enterprise workspaces can choose to store data in the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Virginia). The
region is chosen when the workspace is created. Moving an existing workspace to another region is
a managed migration that takes up to 10 business days and must be requested through the success
manager.

## Law enforcement requests

Driftwood only discloses customer data to law enforcement when legally required, and notifies the
affected customer unless prohibited by law. Support agents must never discuss or confirm the
existence of a law enforcement request; these are always escalated to the legal team.
